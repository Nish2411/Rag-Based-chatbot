# Usage & Safety Policies

## Purpose

This chatbot is built for practice purposes to help users understand concepts from the book *"Introduction to Deep Learning"*.

## Response Policies

- Always prioritize information from the uploaded PDF
- Clearly mention when answering from general knowledge
- Provide page citations when referencing the document
- Refuse to answer harmful, illegal, or unethical requests
- Maintain academic integrity

## Limitations

- Knowledge is limited to the content of `IntroToDeepLearning.pdf`
- May occasionally produce hallucinations (typical of RAG systems)
- Performance depends on quality of text extraction from PDF

## Data Privacy

- All conversations are processed locally (except calls to Gemini API)
- No user data is stored
- Vector store contains only PDF content

## Disclaimer

This project is for learning and demonstration purposes only.
